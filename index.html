06 / Questions, answered

Before we
get started.

FAQ
How long does a website take to build?＋

A simple one-page website may take around 5–10 working days. A larger website can take longer depending on the pages, content, features and how quickly feedback is provided. I'll give you a timeline after understanding the project.

Will my website work on phones?＋

Yes. Responsive design is part of the process, so the layout is planned for mobile, tablet and desktop screens.

Do you work with small businesses?＋

Yes. I welcome enquiries from small businesses, startups, creatives and individuals who need a professional online presence.

Do I need to provide the text and images?＋

Ideally, you provide your business information, logo, images and service details. If you need help organising the content or choosing suitable visuals, we can include that in the project scope.

Are domain name and hosting included?＋

They are not automatically included in the starting prices. I'll explain the available options and any recurring costs before you commit.

How do payments work?＋

After we agree on the scope and quote, I'll outline the payment milestones in writing. Work begins once the agreed initial payment is received.

Can you redesign my existing website?＋

Absolutely. Share your current website and what you'd like to improve. I'll review the scope and recommend a suitable approach.
